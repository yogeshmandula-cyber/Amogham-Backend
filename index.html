OUR STORY

Multi-cuisine, single roof.

Amogham was built around one idea — that a family restaurant should have something for everyone at the table. Tandoori classics sit alongside Indo-Chinese starters, slow-cooked vegetarian curries, and biryani made the traditional dum way.

Every corner of the dining room, from the tufted velvet booths to the warm brass lighting, is designed for long meals and easy conversation.
